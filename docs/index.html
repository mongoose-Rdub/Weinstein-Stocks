How to read this

Active buy = Stage 2 breakout on ≥2x volume (or continuation/pullback buy), market and sector not in decline, no heavy overhead resistance, stop within 15% of entry. Entry is the breakout level; do not chase more than 10% above. Stop sits one-eighth below the base floor (or below the pullback low), placed as a sell-stop. Size positions equally, about 1/15 of the account each.
